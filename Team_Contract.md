## Roles and Responsibilities

Primary roles identify each member’s main responsibilities but do not limit contributions to other areas.

### Hannah Krzywkowski — Coordinator / Front-End Lead / Documentation Owner

Hannah will have primary responsibility for:

- **Front-end and UI/UX:** Develop polls, results, statistics, navigation, and final user interfaces.
- **Back-end development:** Develop most server-side features, APIs, application logic, and result processing.
- **Requirements and documentation:** Organize requirements, user stories, the README, and major project documentation.
- **Project coordination:** Track tasks, deadlines, meetings, responsibilities, and unfinished or blocked work.
- **General development:** Contribute to architecture, database development, research, integration, testing, debugging, and code review.

### Aryan Balyan — System Architect / Database Lead

Aryan will have primary responsibility for:

- **System architecture:** Lead technical architecture and maintain system diagrams.
- **Database development:** Lead database design and implementation for polls, responses, results, and other application data.
- **Back-end development:** Develop assigned server-side features, APIs, application logic, and result processing.
- **Technical research and setup:** Lead research and setup for frameworks, databases, hosting, APIs, libraries, and development tools.
- **General development:** Contribute to front-end development, requirements, integration, documentation, testing, debugging, and code review.

Hannah and Aryan will share responsibility for test planning, functional and integration testing, debugging, and code review. Tasks may be reassigned based on workload, difficulty, availability, and project needs.

## Communication and Meetings

Text messaging will be used for routine communication and email for formal or detailed matters. Members will respond within 24 hours on weekdays and provide advance notice of extended unavailability when possible.

The team will meet online at least weekly to review progress, deadlines, tasks, and blockers. Meetings with Professor Bhide will be scheduled as needed, with a goal of at least every two weeks, and will be remote during Fall 2026. Hannah will coordinate scheduling and document key decisions, advisor feedback, and action items.

## Decision-Making and Conflict Resolution

Hannah and Aryan have equal decision-making authority and may make routine decisions within their assigned responsibilities. Decisions affecting requirements, features, architecture, database structure, shared interfaces, privacy, security, or the other member’s work require mutual agreement.

Disagreements will be evaluated based on requirements, feasibility, effort, technical risk, and maintainability. Unresolved technical, design, scope, or feasibility issues will be referred to Professor Bhide for guidance. Course, grading, policy, or serious participation issues will be referred to Professor Zhan.
